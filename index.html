Questions

What brands and creators want to know before they sign up.

What does Smallfold cost?

Smallfold takes a commission on each deal between a brand and a creator. No deal, no commission.

Who counts as a small creator?

A creator with 1,000 to 50,000 followers.

How does a deal on Smallfold work?

A brand posts a campaign brief. Creators apply, the brand approves the ones that fit, and the creators post the ad and get paid.

Is Smallfold for brands or for creators?

Both. Larger companies post campaigns and small creators apply to them.

How do creators get paid?

Through Smallfold. The brand pays the creator's fee there when the deal is done, and Smallfold keeps its commission.
