For creators

A sponsor for your next video

You do not need a manager or an agency. Every sponsorship on Smallfold is an offer from a brand. It says what the brand wants in your video and what it pays.

  • You read the whole offer first

    What the brand wants in the video, the fee and the date are in the offer before you apply.

  • You keep the whole fee

    The brand pays Smallfold's 10% commission on top. Nothing is taken from your fee.

  • You choose what you promote

    You pick the offers you apply to. No offer is assigned to you.

How it works

A sponsorship in four steps

  1. Join with your channel

    Sign up as a creator and add your YouTube channel.

  2. Apply to a sponsorship

    Read the offers from brands and apply to the ones that suit what you make.

  3. Confirm and publish

    When the brand approves you, confirm the fee and the date, then publish the video.

  4. Get paid

    The brand pays your fee through Smallfold once the video is published.

Questions

Before you join

What does Smallfold cost?

Smallfold takes a 10% commission on each deal, and the brand pays it. As a creator you keep the whole fee in the offer. No deal, no commission.

Is my channel big enough?

Yes, if it has between 1,000 and 50,000 subscribers. A channel with fewer than 1,000 cannot join yet: come back when you reach 1,000.

How do I get paid?

Through Smallfold. The brand pays your fee there once your video is published.

Can I say no to a sponsorship?

Yes. You choose which offers to apply to, and you can read each one in full first. Nothing is assigned to you.
