A sponsor chosen for your channel

You do not need a manager or an agency, and you do not search through a list. You tell us about your channel, and we look for a sponsor that suits your videos and the people who watch them.

  • We start with your channel

    We look at what you make and who watches it before we suggest a sponsor.

  • You keep the whole fee

    The sponsor pays Smallfold's 10% commission on top. Nothing is taken from your fee.

  • You decide

    You can say no to any sponsor we suggest. Nothing is agreed without you.
