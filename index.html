For creators

A sponsor for your next video

You do not need a manager or a media kit. Every sponsorship on Smallfold is a brief from a brand: read it, and apply if it fits your audience.

  • In every brief

    What the brand wants

    A short mention, a dedicated video or a link in the description. The brief says which.

  • In every brief

    What it pays

    The fee is written in the brief before you apply, so you know what the work is worth.

  • In every brief

    When it is due

    Each brief has a publish-by date. You agree it with the brand in one message thread.

How it works

A sponsorship in four steps

  1. Join with your channel

    Sign up as a creator and add your YouTube channel.

  2. Apply to a sponsorship

    Pick the briefs that suit what you make and apply.

  3. Agree and publish

    Settle the fee and the date with the brand, then publish the video.

  4. Get paid

    The brand pays your fee through Smallfold when the deal is done.

For brands

Sponsor many small channels with one brief

  • Post a sponsorship brief

    Say what you sell, what you want in the video and what you pay.

  • Compare the channels that apply

    Each application shows the channel's topic and subscriber count.

  • Pay per finished deal

    You pay each creator's fee through Smallfold. Smallfold takes a commission on each deal.

Questions

Before you join

What does Smallfold cost?

Smallfold takes a commission on each deal between a brand and a creator. No deal, no commission.

Is my channel big enough?

Yes, if it has between 1,000 and 50,000 subscribers. Smallfold is built for channels of that size.

How do I find a sponsor?

Join as a creator, add your channel and apply to the open sponsorships that fit your audience. The brand approves the channels it wants to work with.

How do I get paid?

Through Smallfold. The brand pays your fee there when the deal is done, and Smallfold keeps its commission.

Who are the sponsors?

Larger companies that want small creators to advertise their products.
