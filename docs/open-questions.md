# Open questions for the owner

The team did not guess any of these. Until each is answered, the site either leaves the
point out or shows a clearly marked blank. `README.md` says where each answer goes.

## Needed before the site goes public

| Question | What the site does today |
|---|---|
| Which form service will deliver the forms, and what is its form address? | Both forms show "not connected yet" and send nothing. |
| What is the site's address (domain)? | No canonical links, no sitemap, and shared links show no picture. |
| What is the legal name of the business, and in which country? | Marked blanks in Privacy and Terms, including the governing law. |
| Who checks the Privacy and Terms drafts? | Both pages carry a draft notice. |
| What is the phone number? | The site shows the email only. |

## Would make the site more convincing

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| How and when is a creator paid, and how is the fee agreed and put in writing? | Creators ask this first. The site says only that the commission comes out of the fee once a deal is done. Terms has blanks for it. |
| Does the commission rate apply to the whole fee? | The tiers are shown as given, with no worked example. |
| Is every channel in the subscriber range accepted, and what does the reply to an application say? | The site promises a reply within 2 working days and nothing about the outcome. |
| Is a sponsor promised, and how long does a match usually take? | The site promises neither. Terms says no sponsor is guaranteed, as a draft clause. |
| How quickly do brands get a reply? | The brand page says "we will write back" with no time. |
| Why is a phone number asked for, and will you call? | The form marks it optional and does not say. |
| What do you tell a sponsor about a creator, and a creator about a sponsor? | A marked blank in Privacy. |
| What happens when a channel grows past 50,000 subscribers? | Not mentioned. |
| How long are applications kept, and who is the contact for privacy requests? | Marked blanks in Privacy. |
| Can you name real sponsors or show a creator who was paid? | The site shows only invented examples, labelled as examples. |
| Do you have photographs of your own? | The eight pictures are drawn, not photographed. `docs/image-credits.md` lists four free photographs that could replace some. |

## Statements the team added that go slightly beyond the brand facts

Check that you are happy with each:

- "So we look at the small ones." (home page statement, About title and description)
- "Nothing goes ahead without your yes." and "A deal goes ahead only when you and the sponsor both say yes."
- "Finding the sponsor is our work."
- "Apply once yours reaches 1,000." (for channels under 1,000 subscribers)
- In Privacy: that the inbox is a Gmail account provided by Google, and that Google receives a visitor's IP address when the fonts load.
- In Terms: "We do not promise to find a sponsor for every channel, or a channel for every sponsor", that details in an application must be true, and that the terms may change.

## Not tested

The site was tested in one browser engine (Chromium) on simulated devices. Before launch,
someone should open it in Safari on an iPhone and a Mac, in Firefox, and on a mid-range
Android phone, and try the menu, both forms and the scroll effects. Nobody has used it with
a screen reader, and the forms have never been sent to a real form service.
