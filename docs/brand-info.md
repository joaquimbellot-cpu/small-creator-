# Smallfold: brand information

This file is the single source of truth for every brand fact on the website.
If a fact is not written here, it must not appear on the site. Ask the owner instead.

Status: **confirmed by the owner on 20 November 2026**, including the three carried-over points in section 11.

## 1. The brand

| | |
|---|---|
| Name | **Smallfold** (one word) |
| Tagline | **A sponsor chosen for your channel** |
| What it does | Smallfold finds sponsors for small YouTube channels. A person looks at each channel and chooses a sponsor that suits it. |
| Voice | A company speaking as "we". No founder, team member or other person is named or shown. |
| Tone | Premium and calm: quiet, polished, a little exclusive, the way a boutique agency writes. Plain words, short sentences. |
| Language | English only |

### Story (use this and nothing more)

> Smallfold was started by a YouTube creator with a small channel who found that sponsors only looked at big ones.

Do not add a name, a channel, a date, a place or any other detail to this story.

## 2. Audience

- **First:** creators with a small YouTube channel. The home page speaks to them.
- **Second:** brands that want to sponsor small channels. They have their own page.

## 3. What makes Smallfold different

Only these three points may be presented as differences:

1. **Matched by hand.** A person looks at each channel and picks the sponsor. No automatic list, no algorithm.
2. **Only small channels.** Smallfold works only with channels that have 1,000 to 50,000 subscribers.
3. **The creator has the final say.** A creator can turn down any sponsor that is suggested.

Not a selling point: "creators keep the whole fee". It is no longer true (see pricing).

## 4. Service and pricing

- **Platform:** YouTube now. Other platforms will come later; the site may say so in one line, without naming them or giving a date.
- **Channel size:** 1,000 to 50,000 subscribers.
- **Who pays:** the creator. The commission is taken from the creator's fee. **The brand pays nothing extra**: it pays exactly the agreed fee.
- **When:** only when a deal is done. Joining is free.

| Size of the sponsorship | Smallfold's commission |
|---|---|
| Under $500 | 10% |
| $500 to $1,999 | 8% |
| $2,000 or more | 5% |

- **Example deals:** allowed, but every one must be clearly labelled as an example, and **no dollar amounts** may be shown in them.
- **How the money moves:** not decided. The site may only say that the commission comes out of the fee once a deal is done. It must not describe who pays whom, when, or by what method.

## 5. Goal of the site

- **Main goal:** creators apply to join.
- **Main button:** "Apply".
- **Reply time:** Smallfold replies to an application within 2 working days.
- **Brands:** send a short enquiry from the brands page.
