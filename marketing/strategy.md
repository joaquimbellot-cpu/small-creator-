# Smallfold creator campaign: strategy

Source of facts: `marketing/brand-brief.md` (draft, awaiting owner confirmation, 23 November 2026). Audience pains and objections below are reasoning, not research. They must never be shown as data or statistics in any ad.

**Goal:** applications. **One call to action:** Apply (https://smallfold.netlify.app/apply).
**Platforms:** YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels (9:16, 15 to 30 s), Instagram feed (1:1 and 9:16 static). English.

**Rules that apply to every line below**
- No money promise. No "earn", "get paid", "make money".
- No numbers of creators, sponsors, deals or matches. No testimonials. No named brands or creators.
- No promise of a sponsor and no time to match.
- No digs at big channels, agencies or platforms.
- No slang, memes or trends. No invented urgency. No exclamation marks, no superlatives.
- The on-screen person is an AI presenter. They say "you" and "Smallfold". They never say "I" about owning a channel, getting a sponsor or earning anything.

---

## 1. Audience

### Who they are
People who run a YouTube channel with 1,000 to 50,000 subscribers. They make videos on their own or with one or two helpers. They have a channel they care about, and many have little time to spare for anything other than the videos. Channels under 1,000 subscribers cannot join yet. Brands are not addressed in this campaign.

### Pains (reasoning, not data)

| # | Pain | Plain observation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sponsors feel out of reach | Many small creators feel sponsorships are for bigger channels. |
| 2 | Searching takes time away from making videos | Many small creators feel that finding sponsors is a second job on top of the first. |
| 3 | Cold emails feel awkward | Many small creators do not know who to write to or what to say. |
| 4 | Fear of the wrong fit | Many small creators worry a sponsor will not suit their audience or their voice. |
| 5 | Fear of losing control | Many small creators worry that a deal will be pushed on them. |
| 6 | Doubt about being noticed | Many small creators feel nobody is looking at channels of their size. |

### Objections to applying, and the true answer

| Objection | True fact from the brief |
|---|---|
| "My channel is too small." | Smallfold is for channels with 1,000 to 50,000 subscribers. Only small channels. |
| "This will cost me money up front." | Joining is free. No deal, no commission. |
| "I will be stuck with a sponsor I do not like." | You can turn down any sponsor we suggest. Nothing goes ahead without your yes. |
| "It is just an automatic list." | A person looks at your channel and chooses a sponsor. No automatic list, no algorithm. |
| "It will be a lot of work to apply." | The form asks for your name, email, a link to your channel, what it is about and your subscriber count. A phone number is optional. We reply within 2 working days. |

## 2. Angles

Leader's ruling on voice: hooks that say "we" or "our" are for on-screen text, captions and ad copy only. When a presenter or voice-over speaks them, "we" becomes "Smallfold" ("Smallfold looks at the small ones.").

Five angles. Each hook is 10 words or fewer and can be spoken in about 2 seconds.

### Angle 1: A Person Picks
**Idea:** A real person looks at your channel and chooses the sponsor, by hand.
**Rests on:** A person chooses each sponsor by hand. No automatic list, no algorithm. "Matched by hand." "Chosen by hand, one channel at a time."
**Hooks**
1. A person chooses a sponsor to suggest. Not a list.
2. No algorithm. Someone reads about your channel.
3. Each sponsor is chosen by hand.

**Best format:** talking-presenter video.

### Angle 2: Your Yes
**Idea:** You stay in charge. Nothing happens unless you say yes.
**Rests on:** You say yes or no. You can turn down any sponsor we suggest. Nothing goes ahead without your yes. "You have the final say." The sponsor also approves each channel.
**Hooks**
1. Nothing goes ahead without your yes.
2. You can turn down any sponsor we suggest.
3. You have the final say.

**Best format:** static.

### Angle 3: No Deal, No Commission
**Idea:** It costs nothing to join, and Smallfold is paid only when a deal is done.
**Rests on:** Joining is free. No deal, no commission. The commission is charged only once a deal is done. The sponsor pays the agreed fee and nothing extra.
**Hooks**
1. No deal, no commission.
2. Joining is free. No deal, no commission.
3. Joining is free. Commission comes only after a deal.

**Best format:** static. Works as voice-over too.
**Note:** The 10%, 8% and 5% bands may appear only when explaining the commission, and only once the owner confirms (see section 6).

### Angle 4: We Look at the Small Ones
**Idea:** Smallfold exists for channels of your size, and it was started by a small channel.
**Rests on:** For channels with 1,000 to 50,000 subscribers. "Only small channels." "We look at the small ones." Founder line: started by a YouTube creator with a small channel, who found that sponsors only looked at big ones. "Created by a small channel, for small channels." Tell the founder line as plain fact, with no mocking.
**Hooks**
1. We look at the small ones.
2. 1,000 to 50,000 subscribers? This is for you.
3. Created by a small channel, for small channels.

**Best format:** voice-over video.

### Angle 5: Our Work, Not Yours
**Idea:** You make the videos. Finding sponsors is Smallfold's job.
**Rests on:** You do not look for sponsors yourself. "Finding the sponsor is our work." You apply with a link, a topic and a subscriber count.
**Hooks**
1. Finding the sponsor is our work.
2. You do not have to look for sponsors.
3. Finding sponsors is our work, not yours.

**Best format:** static. Works as talking-presenter too.

## 3. Recommended test batch

### Video concepts (clearly different)

| Concept | Angle | Format | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Concept A** | 1. A Person Picks | Talking-presenter video. The AI presenter speaks to camera in the warm-lamp room and walks through three steps: you apply, a person chooses, you say yes or no. | It answers the biggest doubt (a list or an algorithm) with Smallfold's clearest difference, and a face helps explain the steps. |
| **Concept B** | 4. We Look at the Small Ones | Voice-over video. No one talks to camera. ### Static ads

| Static | Angle and headline | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Static 1** | Angle 3. "No deal, no commission." | It meets the cost objection head on in four words. |

| **Static 3** | Angle 5. "Finding the sponsor is our work." | It speaks to the time and effort pain, and stays separate from the two angles used in the videos. |

All five angles are covered once. Supporting lines allowed next to the button: "Joining is free." / "We reply within 2 working days." / "For channels with 1,000 to 50,000 subscribers."

## 4. Platform plan

| | YouTube Shorts | Instagram Reels | Instagram feed |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Format** | 9:16 video | 9:16 video | Static, 1:1 and 9:16 |
| **Length** | 15 to 30 s. Aim for the shorter end so the Apply line is not cut off. | 15 to 30 s. Same cut as Shorts. | Not applicable. One image. |
| **First frame** | Hook line on screen and spoken at once. The presenter or the first image is in view from frame 1. | Same. Keep key text away from the top and bottom edges, where the app's buttons and caption sit. | The headline is the first thing seen. One line, large serif type on cream. |
| **Captions on screen** | Always. Many people watch with sound off. Short lines, high contrast, in the brand type. | Always. Same captions as Shorts. | Not applicable. The headline and one supporting line carry the message. |
| **Where Apply sits** | Say "Apply" in the last 3 to 5 seconds, show an end card with the button wording, and use the platform's link or button. Put the link in the description. | Say "Apply" in the last 3 to 5 seconds, show an end card, and use the ad's button. Keep the end card clear of the bottom area the app covers. | "Apply" as a small button-style label on the image, plus the ad's own button and link. |
| **AI-content label** | Owner turns on YouTube's "altered or synthetic content" setting before publishing. | Owner turns on Instagram's "AI info" label before publishing. | Same. Owner turns on the "AI info" label before publishing. |

Notes for all placements:
- The same two videos serve both Shorts and Reels. Check each export in the app's safe areas before publishing.
- The AI presenter must be disclosed through the platform label. The owner publishes. Nothing is posted by this team.
- No logo and no brand name inside generated images or video. The real logo is placed on afterwards.

## 5. What to measure

These are definitions only. Targets are set by the owner after the test.

| Measure | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Hook rate** | 3-second views divided by impressions. It shows how many people stayed past the opening. Used to compare hooks and first frames. |
| **Click-through rate (CTR)** | Clicks on the Apply link divided by impressions. It shows how many people who saw the ad wanted to go to the form. |
| **Cost per application** | Total ad spend divided by the number of submitted applications. It shows what each application cost. |

How to read them together: hook rate compares openings, CTR compares messages and end cards, cost per application is the final judge. Compare ads against each other within the test, not against outside numbers.

## 6. Open points for the owner

1. The brief is still a draft. Founder line, "We reply within 2 working days", and the commission rates (10%, 8%, 5%) are all waiting for confirmation. Ads should not use them until confirmed.
2. Voice: section 7 of the brief says "you and we", but the presenter rule says the presenter speaks about "Smallfold". Scripts should use "Smallfold" for the presenter and keep "we" for text on screen only if the owner agrees. Hooks above that use "we" are for on-screen text or may be rewritten as "Smallfold". **Settled by the team leader:** see the ruling at the top of section 2.
3. The tagline "A sponsor chosen for your channel" and "Finding the sponsor is our work" could be read as a promise of a sponsor. Scripts should add that applying does not promise a sponsor, or use wording that says the match is chosen when a sponsor is found.
4. The brief gives no placement for the commission bands in a short video. They are best left out of the 15 to 30 s videos and kept to one static or the landing page.
