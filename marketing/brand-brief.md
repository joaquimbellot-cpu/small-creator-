# Smallfold brand brief

**Status: draft, waiting for the owner's confirmation (23 November 2026).**

This file is the only source of facts for every script, image and ad. If a claim is not written here, it does not go into an ad. Every fact below was taken from the live website's own wording (`src/content/`).

## 1. What Smallfold is

- Smallfold finds sponsors for small YouTube channels. A person chooses each sponsor by hand.
- Tagline: **"A sponsor chosen for your channel"**
- One-line description: "Smallfold finds sponsors for small YouTube channels, each chosen by a person."
- Footer line: "Sponsors for small YouTube channels, matched by hand."
- Smallfold is online only.

## 2. Facts we may state

Each line is a fact from the website. Ads may reword them, but must not add to them.

**Who it is for**
- YouTube channels with 1,000 to 50,000 subscribers.
- A channel with fewer than 1,000 subscribers cannot join yet.
- YouTube only, for now. Other platforms will come later.

**How a match is made**
1. You apply: a link to your channel, what it is about, and your subscriber count.
2. A person looks at your channel and chooses a sponsor that suits it. No automatic list, no algorithm.
3. You say yes or no. You can turn down any sponsor we suggest. The sponsor also approves each channel.
4. When both sides say yes, the deal goes ahead.

**What the creator can count on**
- You do not look for sponsors yourself. Finding one is Smallfold's work.
- Nothing goes ahead without your yes.
- We reply within 2 working days of an application.

**Cost**
- Joining is free.
- No deal, no commission. The commission is charged only once a deal is done.
- The commission comes out of the creator's fee: 10% on a sponsorship under $500, 8% from $500 to $1,999, 5% at $2,000 or more.
- The larger the sponsorship, the lower the rate.
- The sponsor pays the agreed fee and nothing extra.

**Where Smallfold comes from**
- "Smallfold was started by a YouTube creator with a small channel who found that sponsors only looked at big ones."
- "Created by a small channel, for small channels."

**The application form asks for:** name, email, a link to the channel, what the channel is about, subscriber count. A phone number is optional.

## 3. What we must never say

Smallfold has no published results. So ads never state or hint at any of these:

- A number of creators, sponsors, brands, deals or matches.
- Any earnings, income, payout or "typical deal" amount. The dollar figures in section 2 are commission bands only and may appear only when explaining the commission.
- Any testimonial, quote, review, rating or success story. There are none.
- Any named brand partner or named creator.
- That a sponsor is guaranteed, or how long a match takes. Applying does not promise a sponsor.
- That the presenter is a Smallfold customer, a YouTuber, or speaks from experience. The presenter is an AI presenter who explains the service.
- The three match examples on the website (baking channel and flour mill, bicycle restoration and tool maker, houseplants and pot shop) are invented. If an ad uses one, it must carry the word "Example" on screen.

Also out, from the owner's interview:

- Any money promise ("earn", "get paid", "make money from your channel").
- Digs at big channels, other agencies or platforms, by name or by hint. The founder line in section 2 may be told as plain fact, without mocking anyone.
- Slang, memes and trend formats.
- Invented scarcity or deadlines ("limited spots", "closing soon"). They would be untrue.
- Real people's faces or voices, imitations of real YouTubers, other companies' logos (including the YouTube logo), copyrighted characters or music.

## 4. Audience for this campaign

- **Who:** people who run a YouTube channel with 1,000 to 50,000 subscribers.
- **Brands** are a later round and are not addressed here.

The audience's problems (pains, objections, angles) are the Strategy Agent's work and go in `strategy.md`. They are reasoning, not facts, and ads must not present them as research or statistics.

## 5. Goal and call to action

- **Goal:** applications from creators.
- **The single call to action:** **Apply**
- **Landing page:** https://smallfold.netlify.app/apply
- Supporting lines allowed next to the button: "Joining is free." / "We reply within 2 working days." / "For channels with 1,000 to 50,000 subscribers."

## 6. Platforms and formats

- YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels: video, 9:16, 15 to 30 seconds.
- Instagram feed: static images, 1:1 and 9:16.
- Later versions: 1:1 and 16:9 cuts of the approved videos.
- Language: English only.

## 7. Voice

- **Friendly and plain-spoken, with a bold, punchy hook.** The first two seconds may be direct and confident. The rest sounds like the website: calm, warm, honest.
- Short sentences. Everyday words. Say "you" and "we".
- State things flatly and let them stand: "No deal, no commission."
- No exclamation marks, no superlatives ("best", "easiest", "#1").

**Wording bank (taken from the site, safe to reuse as written)**

- "Chosen by hand, one channel at a time"

## 8. Look

**Colours**

| Name | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Cream | `#F1EFEB` | Main background |
| Ink | `#0E0E0E` | Text on cream, dark backgrounds |
| Paper | `#FAF8F4` | Cards and raised areas on cream |
| Ink soft | `#1A1A19` | Raised areas on ink |
| Muted | `#5C5A55` | Secondary text on cream |
| Pen blue | `#2B44D6` | The one accent on cream |
| Pen blue on ink | `#A8B4FF` | The same accent on dark backgrounds |

Use one accent only. Blue appears as a small detail, never as a background.

**Type**
- Headlines: Newsreader (serif), light or regular weight. Italic for a single stressed word.
- Small text and buttons: Instrument Sans, medium or semibold.

**Logo**
- Files: `src/assets/logo/` (`logo.svg`, `logo-on-ink.svg`, `symbol.svg`, `symbol-on-ink.svg`).
- The symbol is a thin ring holding a serif "S", with a small pen-blue dot at the upper right.
- Image models garble logos. Generated images and videos must contain **no logo and no brand name**. The real logo file is placed on top afterwards.

**Photography style (the website's pictures, all generated with Higgsfield)**
- Dim, quiet rooms lit by one warm lamp. Film grain. Muted browns, creams and blacks.
- One small blue object in the frame: a blue notebook or a blue pen.
- People are shown from behind or as hands only. No faces on the website.
- Objects: a camera on a small tripod, a wooden desk, blank cards laid out on a table with a hand choosing one.
- Illustration: fine thread lines (`src/assets/illustrations/threads.svg`).

For UGC-style videos the presenter does show a face, but the room keeps this look: warm lamp, wood, cream wall, one blue object.

## 9. AI and disclosure

- All pictures, video, voices and presenters are made with Higgsfield.
- The owner turns on the platform's AI-content label when publishing (YouTube's "altered or synthetic content" setting, Instagram's "AI info" label).
- The presenter never says "I" about owning a channel, getting a sponsor or earning anything.
