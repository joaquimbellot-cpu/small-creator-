# Concept B: "We look at the small ones."

**Format:** Voice-over only, calm and warm. No one speaks to camera. Quiet lamp-lit desk and hands shots in the website's style, 9:16: wooden desk, camera on a small tripod, blank cards, one blue notebook or blue pen. No faces.
**Target length:** 23 seconds (hard range 15 to 30). Spoken words: **53** (H1), 55 (H2), 55 (H3).
