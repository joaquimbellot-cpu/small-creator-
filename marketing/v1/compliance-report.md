## 1. Verdict

The campaign is close to ready but is not clean yet. The copy is disciplined: no money promises, no invented numbers, testimonials, partners or urgency, no exclamation marks, no superlatives, and the commission rates appear only in the long ad text, worded as in the brief. Every spoken line in `generation-sheet.md` matches the scripts word for word, and the sheet does say `use_unlim: true`, stop if anything costs credits, and never publish. The spoken lines never use "I" or "we". All six statics and all 13 review frames carry the logo, undistorted and with correct spelling, and I found no stray readable text, other company's logo or recognisable real person in the pictures. Two things must be fixed before anything is published. First, the spoken and on-screen line "Finding sponsors takes time from your videos." is a claim about creators' lives that is not in brief section 2. Second, the end cards of both videos have no "Made with AI" label, although the scripts and sheet require one. The warnings are mostly small: text and labels that are too small for a phone, a few strategy hooks that hint at a guaranteed sponsor or add a second call to action, a few safe-zone margins that are only just met, and some documents that no longer match the built pictures.

## 3. What passed

**A. Text files**
- No exclamation marks anywhere in `strategy.md`, `scripts/`, `ad-copy.md`, `creative-direction.md` or `generation-sheet.md`. No superlatives, no "earn / get paid / make money", no urgency or scarcity wording.
- No numbers of creators, sponsors, deals or matches; no testimonials, ratings, named partners or named creators; no digs at big channels, agencies or platforms; no slang or memes.
- Spoken lines (Concept A all three hooks and body; Concept B all three hooks and body) use "you" and "Smallfold" and never "I" or "we". The presenter is always described as an AI presenter and never as a creator, customer or staff member, with no personal results.
- Commission percentages appear only in the long ad text (`ad-copy.md`), worded as in the brief. They are absent from videos, statics, scripts and strategy hooks.
- Single call to action "Apply" in scripts, statics and ad copy. Supporting lines used are the three allowed ones, plus "Applying does not promise a sponsor."
- Word counts check out (Concept A 57 words, Concept B H1 53 words). Ad headline and link-description character counts match the stated counts and are within the 40 and 30 character limits. Caption character counts (107, 136, 110, 227) are correct.
- `generation-sheet.md`: every spoken line (Concept A clip 1 and clip 2, Concept B voice-over H1, H2 and H3, Concept A hooks H2 and H3) matches the scripts word for word. Rule 2 says `use_unlim: true` on every generation request. Rule 3 says to STOP and tell the owner if anything would cost credits (see warning 11 for the gap). Rule 4 says never publish or post. Rule 5 and each prompt exclude logos, text and captions; rule 7 excludes real likeness, brand names, readable text and copyrighted music. Rule 6 forbids rewriting the spoken lines.
- Generation prompts contain no logo, brand name, readable text, real-person likeness, YouTube logo or copyrighted music or characters. The camera is specified as blank-bodied.

**B. On-screen text (`statics.json`, `videos.json`)**
- All headlines, sub-lines and footers are from the brief's facts or wording bank. No spelling errors. No exclamation marks, numbers of creators, money promises or testimonials.
- "1,000 to 50,000 subscribers" appears correctly. "Joining is free." and "No deal, no commission." are correct. No match example is used, so no "Example" label is needed.
- Only exception: the beat 2 line in warning/fail 1 and the end-card headline in warning 3.

**C. Built pictures**
- All 6 statics (1:1 and 9:16): logo top left, crisp and undistorted; "Image made with AI" top right; correct spelling; headline, sub-line, Apply button and footer all visible, not clipped, with good contrast against the dark veil (except the points in warnings 4 to 7); layout and type match the brand (Newsreader serif headline with one stressed phrase in pen blue, Instrument Sans for small text, "Apply" button).
- Video frames a-1 to a-6 and b-1 to b-5: logo and "Made with AI" present, text correct, not clipped, no overlap with other text, large enough to read (headline about 36 to 52 px on 540 wide frames; sub-lines about 17 px). Captions sit between about 60% and 78% of the frame height, clear of the bottom 15%.
- Pictures: no extra fingers or warped hands (the hand with the pen in a-1 and b-4 is dark but plausible, two fingernails and the thumb visible, no fusing); no logo, brand name or other company's mark found (apart from the small camera badge in warning 13); no identifiable face (people are seen from behind or as hands only); no YouTube logo; no copyrighted characters; no stray lettering on cards, notebooks or walls.
- Videos are silent, as stated, so there are no voice or music issues.
