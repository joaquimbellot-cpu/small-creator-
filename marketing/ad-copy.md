# Smallfold ad copy

Source of facts: `marketing/brand-brief.md`. Voice on screen and in ad text is the brand's own ("we" allowed). One call to action everywhere: **Apply**. Link: https://smallfold.netlify.app/apply
Rules kept: no money promise, no counts of creators, sponsors or deals, no testimonials, no named brands, no guarantee of a sponsor, no exclamation marks, no superlatives. The owner turns on the platform AI-content label when publishing. No logo or brand name inside generated images; the real logo is placed on afterwards.

## 1. Static ads

Layout for all three (as built in `marketing/assets/static/`): one of the website's own lamp-lit Higgsfield pictures fills the frame under a dark veil, the headline is in Newsreader (serif, light) with one stressed phrase in pen blue, the button "Apply" is in Instrument Sans, and a small footer line sits beside it. The real logo is top left and the label "Image made with AI" is top right. Each ad exists in 1:1 and 9:16.

## 3. Main texts

**Short (about 125 characters)** (110 characters)

A person looks at your channel and chooses a sponsor to suggest. You can turn it down. Joining is free. Apply.

**Medium (about 250 characters)** (227 characters)

We look at the small ones. Smallfold is for YouTube channels with 1,000 to 50,000 subscribers. A person looks at your channel and chooses a sponsor to suggest. You say yes or no. Joining is free. We reply within 2 working days.

**Long (about 500 characters, includes the commission bands as worded in the brief)** (493 characters)

A person looks at your channel and chooses a sponsor to suggest. No automatic list, no algorithm. You say yes or no, and nothing goes ahead without your yes.

Joining is free. No deal, no commission. The commission comes out of the creator's fee: 10% on a sponsorship under $500, 8% from $500 to $1,999, 5% at $2,000 or more. The sponsor pays the agreed fee and nothing extra.

For channels with 1,000 to 50,000 subscribers. We reply within 2 working days. Applying does not promise a sponsor.
