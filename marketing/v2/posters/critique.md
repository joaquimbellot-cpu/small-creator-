## 1. Verdict

The batch is close. The campaign idea works: every poster carries the same footer, the same logo, the exact support line ("Got 1,000 to 50,000 subs? Apply for a sponsor. It's free.") and "Applying doesn't promise a sponsor.", and the blank is empty on all eight. I found no money amounts, results, reviews, urgency words, company story, other company's name, or anything that copies the YouTube logo or play button. No headline says "you will get a sponsor". The problems are craft problems and two clarity problems, not honesty failures.

Real problems:
- One 9:16 poster (03) breaks the top safe zone.
- Two posters (06, 07) draw the blank in light blue on a light surface, where it nearly disappears.
- Poster 07 has a drawing glitch and a plinth that breaks its own floor.
- Poster 08 does not pass the one-second test; I recommend cutting or reworking it.
- Poster 05 is clever but slow, and its empty bottom section can read as a dig at channels under 1,000 subscribers.

The word "sponsor" is readable at a glance on 01, 02, 04, 05, 07 and 08. It is small on 03 and 06 (readable, but not the hero).

## 3. Run first, and the weakest

Run first (4:5 first, then the matching 9:16 after fix 3 and 15):
1. **02 Monument.** The cleanest one-second read: "this video is sponsored by ____" with a huge empty bar. Sponsor is readable from across a room. Only the widow fix is needed.
2. **04 Checklist.** The idea needs no explanation (three ticks, one empty box) and "FIRST SPONSOR" is huge. It also states the reader's story honestly: there is a gap, and you can apply for it. Fix the small label.
3. **01 Not included.** SPONSOR is the biggest word in the whole batch, and the format joke lands fast. Run it after the blank is given a caption (fix 9). Reserve: 06 Name tag, once the blank colour and strap overlap are fixed.

Weakest: **08 Packshot.** A white box is a picture of nothing; the one-second read is "a box", not "a sponsor offer". The blank is detached from the picture, and "Today's" breaks the no-urgency spirit. If the team wants to keep it, rework as in fix 1; otherwise cut it and run seven.

Series: the set reads as one campaign through the shared footer, the blank bar, the condensed type and the flat colour. Colour is slightly repetitive (maroon on 01 and 08; yellow on 02, 03, 05), which is fine for a series but means 01 and 08 look like the same poster at thumbnail size. Another reason to cut 08.
