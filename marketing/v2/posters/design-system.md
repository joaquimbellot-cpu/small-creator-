# Poster series design system: "The Blank"

Reference build: `src/_template.html` (tokens + footer), `src/proof-a|b|c.html` (generated by `tools/build-proofs.cjs`), rendered by `tools/render.cjs` to `out/`.

## 1. The idea

A flat, loud colour field, one enormous piece of condensed type, and one thick pen-blue bar that is the only blue on the sheet. Everything else is small and quiet, set like the credits and legal line of a real printed poster, so the blank is the thing the eye lands on in one second. It should look like a cinema one-sheet or a street poster pinned to a wall: ink on colour, big margins, nothing floating.

Never: stickers or caption plates on photographs; app screens, cards, chips, pills, buttons or any interface look; video-thumbnail composition (face + arrow + yellow text outline); stock-ad gloss (smiling laptop people, gradients, drop-shadow glows). No play button, no YouTube mark, no emoji, no gradients.

## 2. Grid and margins

| | 4:5 (1080 x 1350) | 9:16 (1080 x 1920) |
|---|---|---|
| Side margins | 64 | 64 |
| Content width | 952 | 952 |
| Top margin | 64 | 280 (safe zone top 14% = 269) |
| Bottom edge of footer | 1286 (64 margin) | 1536 (safe zone: bottom 20% = 384 stays empty colour) |
| Columns | 12, gutter 24 (col = 52.7) | same |
| Baseline unit | 8 px; vertical gaps are multiples of 8 | same |

- Everything is left-aligned to x = 64 and right-aligned to x = 1016. No centred headlines.
- 9:16: only background colour (or a full-bleed colour block, as in proof C) may occupy the top 269 and bottom 384 px. Text, logo, blank and footer stay inside y = 280 to 1536.
- Implemented as CSS tokens `--W --H --mx --top --bottom`; `html.f-9x16` swaps them. `?format=9x16` selects the size.

## 3. Typography (3 families)

| Job | Family | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Display | **Anton** 400 | Tall, tight, heavy: the most "printed poster" face in the set and it fits a big sentence into 952 px. |
| Wit / support line | **DM Serif Display** italic | A human, dry voice against the shouting display; the contrast is the joke. |
| Footer, labels, credits | **Archivo Narrow** 500 / 700 | Narrow and sturdy at 24 px; reads like legal print without turning into a web UI font. |

- Display: ALL CAPS only. Sizes 150 to 226 px for the monument lines (set each line to fit 952 px wide: "THIS VIDEO IS" 198, "SPONSORED" 226), 90 to 200 px for list/billing lines, up to 520 px for a single short word ("SOLO"). Line-height 0.92 (0.78 for a single giant word). Tracking -0.005em (0). Never below 88 px.
- Support line: DM Serif Display italic, 52 px, line-height 1.08, sentence case, always broken as "Got 1,000 to 50,000 subs? / Apply for a sponsor. It's free." Tagline/wit lines 44 to 52 px.
- Labels and credits: Archivo Narrow 700, 28 to 40 px, UPPERCASE, tracking +0.14em to +0.16em.
- Footer: Archivo Narrow 500 24 px (disclaimer, AI note), 700 30 px (Apply line). Minimum text size anywhere: 22 px. Never faux-bold or fake-italic.
- No widows: a headline line is never a single short word dangling unless it is the deliberate "BY" before the blank (give it a full line to itself).

## 4. Colour

Backgrounds are flat. The text colour and the blank colour are fixed per background. Ratios computed WCAG 2.x.

| Name | Background | Text | Text ratio | Blank / Apply | Blank ratio | Logo |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cream | #F1EFEB | ink #0E0E0E | 16.8 | #2B44D6 | 6.3 | logo.svg |
| Signal yellow | #FFD400 | ink #0E0E0E | 13.5 | #2B44D6 | 5.1 | logo.svg |
| Bubblegum | #FFB4C8 | ink #0E0E0E | 11.6 | #2B44D6 | 4.4 | logo.svg |
| Ink | #0E0E0E | cream #F1EFEB | 16.8 | #A8B4FF | 9.8 | logo-on-ink.svg |
| Forest | #0B4A34 | cream #F1EFEB | 8.9 | #A8B4FF | 5.2 | logo-on-ink.svg |
| Oxblood | #7A1426 | cream #F1EFEB | 9.4 | #A8B4FF | 5.4 | logo-on-ink.svg |

Rule: light backgrounds (cream, yellow, bubblegum) use pen blue #2B44D6; dark backgrounds (ink, forest, oxblood) use #A8B4FF. Never mix. Blue appears only as the blank and the "Apply" mark (plus the empty checkbox when it stands in for the blank). Do not use red/orange backgrounds: #2B44D6 drops under 3:1 on them and the blank stops being the loudest thing. A secondary flat shape may borrow another palette colour (proof C uses a yellow block on ink) but the background stays one of the six.

## 5. The blank

- Always a solid pen-blue bar. Never filled in, never outlined, never has text inside, never replaced by underscores.
- Corner radius: 0.04em (about 2 to 9 px); essentially square, slightly softened. Never a pill.
- Inline, text-size (CSS `.blank`): `display:inline-block; width:3.2em; height:0.22em`; the bottom edge sits exactly on the text baseline (an empty inline-block does this natively). With Anton (cap height about 0.82em) that is a bar about 27% of cap height, the weight of a heavy stem.
- At 22 to 40 px text size the bar must still be at least 8 px tall: use `height: max(.22em, 8px)`.
- Fill-the-line (proof C, "SPONSORED BY ____"): flex row, `align-items:baseline`, the bar takes the remaining width, height 0.46em, bottom on the baseline.
- Monument (proof A): the bar is a block of its own on a full 952 px measure, 250 px tall (about 1.2 x the cap height of the line above), directly under "BY", 26 to 30 px gap. It is the largest single shape on the poster.
- Empty-box variant (checklist): 230 px square, 22 px blue border, empty centre.
- The logo and the blank are never adjacent: keep at least 300 px between them.

## 6. Footer strip

Anchored at the bottom of the safe area (bottom edge y = 1286 in 4:5, 1536 in 9:16). Full 952 px wide.

1. 36 px gap above; 3 px rule in text colour, full width; 20 px padding.
2. Row 1 (50 px tall, space-between, vertically centred): left, logo at height 46 px (about 200 px wide); right, "Apply" in Archivo Narrow 700 30 px in blue (#2B44D6 / #A8B4FF), two non-breaking spaces, then "smallfold.netlify.app/apply" in 700 30 px text colour.
3. Row 2 (14 px below, 24 px type, line-height 1.2): left "Applying doesn't promise a sponsor." (500); right, only if a generated picture is used, "Image made with AI" (500, right-aligned to x = 1016).
4. Total height about 150 px including the gap. Logo variant follows the background table.

## 7. Image treatment (used rarely; budget about four pictures)

Two permitted treatments, never mixed on one poster:

- **Cut-out on flat colour.** A transparent-background object, placed on a poster colour, with a drawn hard shadow (offset 24 px right, 24 px down, same silhouette, solid #0E0E0E at 100% on light, or a darker tint of the background on dark; no blur). The object is cropped by the poster edge, not floated: bleed it off at least one side, or sit it on a ruled baseline. It is 55 to 70% of poster width. Type stays in the top or bottom third, never over the object.
- **Full-bleed studio photograph.** An object on a seamless single-colour sweep with hard directional light and a strong shadow, filling the whole canvas; the background colour of the photo is one of the six palette colours (or the nearest printable match). Headline set over the empty part of the sweep, in ink or cream, 150 px or larger. Crop so the object sits on a third line; never centred dead on.
- Add "Image made with AI" to the footer. No faces, no hands holding phones, no logos, no text in the picture, no gradients, no lens flare. Photographs are ordinary objects shot like packshots.

Example Higgsfield prompts:

1. Cut-out: "A single empty white cardboard shipping box, closed, plain, no markings, photographed straight-on at a slight three-quarter angle, hard directional studio light from upper left, crisp shadow edge, isolated on a fully transparent background, no text, no logos, no people."
2. Full-bleed: "Studio packshot of one empty museum plinth, matte white, with a single hard spotlight from above casting a sharp-edged shadow, on a seamless single-colour flat yellow (#FFD400) backdrop, camera low and straight on, plenty of empty space in the upper half, no text, no faces, no logos."
3. Cut-out: "A single plain black wooden clapperboard, completely blank slate with no writing, closed, hard directional light from the right, strong crisp shadow, on a seamless single flat bubblegum pink (#FFB4C8) background, product photography, no text, no people, no logos."

## 8. Layout archetypes

**A. Type monument** (proof A: yellow). One sentence, each line justified to the full 952 px, sizes stepping up toward the blank, blank as the biggest shape. Support line bottom-left above the footer.

```
+--------------------------+
| THIS VIDEO IS            |
| SPONSORED                |
| BY                       |
| [########################]|
| [########################]|
|                          |
| Got 1,000 to 50,000 subs?|
| Apply for a sponsor.     |
|--------------------------|
| logo          Apply  url |
| Applying doesn't promise |
+--------------------------+
```

**B. Object hero.** One cut-out or full-bleed object, headline of 7 words or fewer in the top third in Anton, hard shadow, blank sits in the headline, not on the object.

```
+--------------------------+
| SPONSORED BY [####]      |
|                          |
|        .--------.        |
|        | OBJECT |##      |
|        '--------'##      |
| Got 1,000 ... free.      |
|--------------------------|
| logo   Apply  url        |
| Applying ...   Image made with AI
+--------------------------+
```

**C. Document / parody format** (proof B: checklist on bubblegum; proof C: film one-sheet on ink). The poster is a recognisable printed document: ruled list, billing block, name tag, wanted notice. Items shrink toward the top; the last item (with the blank) is two to three times larger.

```
+--------------------------+       +--------------------------+
| SMALL CHANNEL / TO DO    |       |##########################|
|==========================|       |## S O L O ###############|
| [v] FIRST VIDEO          |       |## a one-person crew #####|
| [v] FIRST COMMENT        |       |==========================|
| [v] FIRST 1,000 SUBS     |       | WRITTEN BY YOU           |
|==========================|       | FILMED BY YOU            |
| [ ]  FIRST               |       | EDITED BY YOU            |
| [ ]  SPONSOR             |       | SPONSORED BY [####]      |
| Got 1,000 ...  free.     |       | Got 1,000 ...  free.     |
| footer                   |       | footer                   |
+--------------------------+       +--------------------------+
```

## 9. Do / do not

1. Do set one thing enormous and everything else at 24 to 52 px.
2. Do keep to the six backgrounds and the fixed text/blue pairings.
3. Do align everything to the 64 px margins and 8 px vertical rhythm.
4. Do keep the blank loud: biggest or boldest blue shape, always on the baseline.
5. Do not fill in, outline, shrink, animate or decorate the blank, or put the logo beside it.
6. Do not put captions, stickers, chips, pills or UI cards on a picture.
7. Do not centre a heading on a plain background or use a face, selfie or play-button shape.
8. Do not set any text under 22 px, put anything in the 9:16 top 14% or bottom 20%, or promise a sponsor, money, results or urgency.
