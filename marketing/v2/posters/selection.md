## Why these

- **Not Included** is the best single line: a phrase everyone knows with one word swapped.
- **The Monument** is the campaign idea at full size, and it is the owner's approved concept (The Line) as a poster.
- **The Film**, **The Checklist**, **The Height Chart** and **The Name Tag** are formats people read in a second: a movie poster, a to-do list, a ride sign, a badge.
- **The Plinth** and **The Packshot** are the two photographs. They are the quiet ones that make the loud ones look louder.

## Left out

- **Not 100K** (P4): the clearest risk of misleading a channel above 50,000 subscribers.
- **Wanted** (P7): "wanted" posters carry a "reward", which would read as money.
- **Dear Sponsor** (P10) and **Picked by Hand** (P12): the gap is less visible than in the others.

## On every poster

- Support line: "Got 1,000 to 50,000 subs? Apply for a sponsor. It's free."
- Footer strip: the real logo, "Apply", "smallfold.netlify.app/apply", "Applying doesn't promise a sponsor.", and "Image made with AI" on posters 7 and 8.
- Two sizes: 1080 x 1350 and 1080 x 1920.
