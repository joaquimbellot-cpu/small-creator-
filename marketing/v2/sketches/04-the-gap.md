## Pitch and promise

- **Pitch:** A cook's video reaches "the sponsor segment" and nothing happens: he holds a TV-host pose at a blank wall while crickets play.
- **Promise:** If your channel has 1,000 to 50,000 subscribers, you can apply for a sponsor, free.
- **Voice:** On-camera speech (cook, lip-synced or dubbed) after 2.5 s of silence; no narrator.
- **Total length:** 20.7 s (17.7 s of story plus a 3.0 s end card).
- **Shots:** 7 rows in the table (some rows are two beats of one generated clip, split where the caption changes). Hard cuts only.
- **Generated clips:** 3, 4 + 8 + 8 s each, 20 s of footage in all (17.7 s used on screen).
- **Signature sound:** Crickets. A looping, slightly too-quiet cricket bed for the 2.5 s of silence (the beat only this ad owns). It cuts the moment he drops the pose. After that, room tone only.

Blank: "____" is always drawn as a thick pen-blue underline (`#2B44D6`) and never filled.

## Two alternative openings for hook tests

1. **Bare wrist.** Open on the cook glancing at his bare wrist while he holds the host pose, no voice, title and labels up, crickets. 0.0 to 2.0, then hard cut to the 2.5 s frozen pose and run on. Avoid any readable watch: the wrist stays bare. All words and their order stay.
2. **Wide set-up.** Open 0.0 to 1.0 wide: the cook strikes the host pose (arm sweeping at the blank wall) as the title appears, then hold the pose for the 2.5 s of silence. This adds 1.0 s and puts the silence second. All words and their order stay.

All captions, labels and the blank stay the same. Changes to the order of spoken words need the owner's approval.

## Risks for AI generation and how the shots avoid them

| Risk | How the design avoids it |
|---|---|
| Silence reads as an error | The title says "(empty)", the caption says "[crickets]", crickets play, the "Pretend" label is up, and the cook blinks and his smile sags. The pose is a joke, not a frozen file. |
| Holding a pose in AI video | Clip A is only 4 s and asks for tiny motion (blink, smile sag) so the face does not drift. If the arm drifts, crop tighter and keep the pose arm off-frame. |
| Soup and liquids | The pot has its lid on and nobody stirs. No pouring, no steam physics asked for. |
| Screens and words | The tripod camera is shown from the side, the back screen never visible. The wall is plain: no calendar, no poster, no writing. The cook wears no watch. |
| Same cook | Beard, rolled sleeves and apron are fixed in one reference still for all three clips. |
| Honesty | The segment stays empty in every frame and the blank wall stays blank. "Pretend" is on from 0.0, 2.5 s before the first spoken word. No deal and no delivered product is shown. |

## Honesty check

- The blank is empty in every frame including the end card.
- "AI actor" is on every row with a person. "Pretend" is on every acted sponsor moment, up before the first spoken word of it.
- The end card carries "Applying doesn't promise a sponsor."
