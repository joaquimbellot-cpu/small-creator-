# One visual style for all five concepts

Built on the "One visual style" section of `selection.md`. The campaign is the opposite of dark and moody: bright daylight rooms, flat colour, big type, hard cuts, one sound idea per ad.

## 1. Light and picture

- Real daylight from a window, white or coloured walls, no shadows deeper than a mid-grey. No dusk, no neon, no vignette, no film grain, no lens flare.
- Rooms are lived in and a little messy (a coloured wall, a ring light, a tripod, a mug). Never a clean studio or a stock office.
- One room per concept. The person and the room are identical in every clip of that concept (one reference still per concept).
- Medium and close shots, locked-off camera. The only camera moves are slow digital push-ins and hard punch-ins made in the edit.
- Leave headroom for the graphics: eyes at about 45% of frame height in 9:16, the person's chest at the caption line. Nothing important sits in the top 10% or the bottom 20%.
- No readable screen, no logo, no lettering anywhere in the filmed picture. Laptops show only the lid, cameras only their side.

## 2. Palette

| Name | Hex | Used for | Contrast note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pen blue | `#2B44D6` | The blank "____", the Apply button, chips (qualifiers such as "1,000 to 50,000 subs") | On cream about 6.3 to 1; cream words on blue the same |
| Pen blue, light | `#A8B4FF` | The same jobs on any dark ink plate or dark footage; never needed on the cream and yellow plates used here | On ink about 9 to 1 |
| Ink | `#0E0E0E` | All text on plates, borders of the labels | Highest contrast on every plate below |
| Cream | `#F1EFEB` | Caption plates, label pills, the end-card background, words on blue | Soft white that does not glare on a phone |
| Sunshine yellow | `#FFD21F` | Accent 1. Plates behind the offer words: "1,000 to 50,000 subs", "Apply for a sponsor. Free.", "Sponsor offer. Free to apply.", the title of The Gap, the label over the empty stand | Ink on it about 14 to 1; blue on it about 5 to 1. The brightest colour in a daylight room, so the offer is the first thing the eye finds |
| Coral | `#FF6B57` | Accent 2. Only for "this is pretend or missing": the "Pretend" label and the "Missing: the sponsor" stamp | Ink on it about 7 to 1. Warm enough to read as a warning-label, orange enough not to look like YouTube red |

Rules: yellow means the offer, coral means pretend or missing, blue means the blank and the action. No other colours on graphics. No gradients, no shadows, no outlines other than the 0.14em ink border on the two label pills and the stamp.

## 3. Type

- One heavy sans-serif for everything (system stack in the sketch page; in production a heavy geometric or grotesque sans at weight 800 to 900, for example Inter or Montserrat ExtraBold). One family, two weights.
- Sentence case only, as written in the boards. No all-caps, no italics, no underline except the blank.
- Sizes are a share of frame height (9:16 frame = 1920 px high):

| Role | Size | Weight | Plate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Headline (offer words, "1,000 to 50,000 subs", "Apply for a sponsor. Free.") | 5.0% (96 px) | 900 | Yellow, ink text |
| Title (The Gap) | 4.4% (84 px) | 900 | Yellow, ink text |
| Stamp ("Missing: the sponsor") | 4.2% (81 px) | 900 | Coral, ink text, rotated 3 degrees left, 0.12em ink border |
| Credit line (The Credits) | 4.4% (84 px) | 900 | Cream, ink text, one line, never wrapped |
| Caption (every spoken line) | 3.4% (65 px) | 800 | Cream, ink text |
| Shelf label (static) | 2.8% to 3.0% | 800 | Cream, ink text; the empty-stand label on yellow |
| Chip | 2.6% (50 px) | 800 | Pen blue, cream text, pill |
| Labels "AI actor", "Pretend", "Made with AI", counters "Take 1" | 2.1% (40 px) | 800 | Pill, 0.14em ink border |
| Small print "Applying doesn't promise a sponsor." | 2.2% (42 px) | 800 | None (on the cream end card) |
| End-card headline | 4.8% (92 px) | 900 | None |
| End-card offer line | 3.6% (69 px) | 900 | None |

- Plates: padding 0.2em top and bottom, 0.42em left and right, corner radius 0.3em. Line height 1.12. No letter spacing.
- 1:1 static: the same roles at larger sizes (headline 6.2%, labels 3.0%, small print 2.8% of the 1080 px height).
- Text column: x 6% to 86%, y 10% to 80% in 9:16. Never text on bare footage; every word is on a plate.

## 4. The blank

- "____" is a thick pen-blue (`#2B44D6`) underline: 0.17em thick, 2.6 characters wide, sitting on the baseline right after the word before it ("Sponsored by ____").
- It is drawn the same way in every concept, on the end card and in the static.
- It is always empty. Nothing is typed over it, nothing replaces it. On the end card a thin caret blinks inside it at 1 Hz (0.5 s on, 0.5 s off) so it reads as waiting, never as filled.
- In The Gap the blank is the empty wall: a pen-blue underline graphic (30% wide, 0.9% high) under the wall patch, from the first frame.
- In Missing Sponsor the blank is the plain white box and the stamp.

## 5. Caption style

- Every spoken line is burned in, in the order spoken, on a cream plate at x 6%, 3.4% high. Top of the block at y 62% (68% where the box or the credits sit above; 69% in The Credits). The bottom of a two-line caption never goes below 80%.
- A caption is one sentence or one phrase of up to 8 words, at most 2 lines.
- Captions pop in over 2 frames (scale 92% to 100%) and leave on a hard cut. No fades, no slides, no karaoke highlight.
- Sound captions in brackets ("[crickets]") are used once, in The Gap.

## 6. Labels

| Label | Where | Size | Look | When |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AI actor | x 6%, y 10.5% | 2.1% of height | Cream pill, 0.14em ink border | On every shot with a person, from the first frame of the shot until the cut. Never on the end card |
| Pretend | x 6%, y 14.3% (directly under AI actor) | 2.1% | Coral pill, ink text, ink border | On every acted sponsor moment. It appears with the first frame of that moment and at least 0.4 s before the first spoken word of it, and stays until the moment ends |
| Made with AI | x 6%, y 10.5% on the end card and the static | 2.1% (9:16), 3.0% (1:1) | Same pill as AI actor | On the end card and on the static; not on the video body |

The owner also switches on each platform's AI-content label when publishing.

## 7. Chip stack and headline slot

Top left, under the labels, a short stack that never moves between shots of one concept: AI actor (y 10.5%), Pretend (y 14.3%, only on acted moments), then the chip "Sponsor offer. Free to apply." or "Free to apply." (y 18.4%, or y 14.3% when there is no Pretend), then "1,000 to 50,000 subs" as a chip (next 4.4% down) or as the yellow headline when the subscriber range is spoken. Nothing in the stack crosses the right 12% line.

## 8. Shared end card (the same in all four videos)

3.0 s, hard cut in, no person, cream `#F1EFEB` background. Left-aligned in the text column. Positions are the top-left corner as % of frame.

| Element | Position (from top-left, % of frame) | Size | Exact words |
|---|---|---|---|
| label | x 6%, y 10.5% | 2.1% of height | "Made with AI" |
| logo | x 6%, y 17% | box 34% wide, 8% high | "LOGO (real file)" |
| end-card headline | x 6%, y 28% | 4.8% of height | "Sponsored by ____" |
| button | x 6%, y 39% | pill 70% wide, 7.5% high; word 4.2% of height | "Apply" |
| offer line | x 6%, y 51% | 3.6% of height | "Free to apply." |
| qualifier | x 6%, y 56% | 2.8% of height | "For YouTube channels with 1,000 to 50,000 subs." |
| small print | x 6%, y 66% | 2.2% of height | "Applying doesn't promise a sponsor." |

- The blank blinks at 1 Hz (caret). Nothing ever fills it.
- Apply button: pen-blue pill, 70% of frame width, 7.5% high, the word "Apply" cream at 4.2% of height. It is the only call to action. The real link is added by the platform's own button; the pill is a graphic.
- Logo: the real logo file, added in the edit (the grey box on the sketch page). Not generated.
- Nothing sits below y 80% or right of x 86% below y 50%.
- Sound: a two-note sting on the cut, then silence.
- No sponsor, no brand, no money, no result, no celebration.

## 9. Cutting pace

- Hard cuts only. No fades, dissolves, whips or transitions.
- A cut or a new caption, chip or headline at least every 2.0 s. A shot that must hold for 3 to 4 s (a caption that long) gets a hard punch-in made in the edit every 2 s (100% to 125% and back).
- Typical shot is 1.4 to 4 s. Longest hold with no cut: 2.6 s (The Gap silence and the silent beat in Missing Sponsor), each with a graphic change inside it.
- Speech never faster than 2.3 words per second.
- Total length with the end card: 20.7 s (The Gap) to 27.2 s (Missing Sponsor).

## 10. Sound signature of each concept

| Concept | Signature sound | What else you hear |
|---|---|---|
| 1. The Line | The pop of the cut-off line: a dry bubble pop on every "by" | Light snap-and-clap beat that builds over the takes and drops out when the creator turns to the viewer; soft tick on each caption pop |
| 2. Missing Sponsor | The hollow knock of the empty box | Kitchen room tone; one small pop on "Something missing?"; no music |
| 3. The Credits | The credits tune stopping dead at 3.9 s | Bouncy original ukulele-and-clap jingle before that; typewriter ticks on credit lines; one "button" note on the end card |
| 4. The Gap | Crickets | Silence in the 2.5 s opening apart from crickets; one spoon clink; room tone after |
| 5. Next First (static) | None | (Optional three dings and a hollow knock if the still is ever animated) |

All sounds are original or sound effects; no licensed music, no real voice, no real person's face.

## 11. Never generated

Words, numbers, logos, screens, packaging print, trophy engravings. All of these are graphics added after the footage is made, from real files. The footage must stay free of readable text so a clean picture can always take any caption.
