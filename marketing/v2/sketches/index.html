Smallfold creator offer: sketch boards

Version 2, five concepts. Rough drawings of what each ad will look like before any video is made. Rows scroll sideways. Every word shown is a graphic that is added afterwards; none will be generated.

Grey blockSomething the AI will film: a person (rounded figure) or a prop. Only the label tells you what it is.
Blue underlineThe blank, "____". It is drawn the same way everywhere and is never filled in.
Dashed red guideSafe zones where phone buttons and captions of the app sit: the top 10%, the bottom 20% and the right edge of the lower half. No words go inside.
Coloured plateWords added in the edit. Yellow plates carry the offer, blue chips carry the qualifiers, coral labels mean "this is pretend". Sizes and positions are real.
