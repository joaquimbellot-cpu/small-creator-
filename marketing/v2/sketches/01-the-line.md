# 1. The Line (score 41), approved: outtakes version

The owner approved this concept with one change: "cut and redo, like it was an error", so it is a reel of outtakes. Source: `selection.md`, section 1.

Vertical 9:16 for YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels. Style: see `style.md`. All words on screen are graphics added in the edit; none is generated.

## Pitch and promise

- **Pitch:** The outtakes of a small creator trying to record "This video is sponsored by". Every take breaks at the same place, because there is no name to say. Then they tell you that you can apply for a sponsor, free.
- **Promise:** If your channel has 1,000 to 50,000 subscribers, you can apply for a sponsor, free.
- **Voice:** On-camera speech (creator, lip-synced or dubbed); no narrator.
- **Total length:** 24.2 s (21.2 s of story plus a 3.0 s end card).
- **Outtake look (graphics and edit, never generated):** a take counter "TAKE 1", "TAKE 2", "TAKE 3" at x 66%, y 10.5% (caps, the one exception to sentence case) and a small red recording dot at x 61.8%, y 10.95% beside it, both during the takes only; jump cuts with a 2-frame white flash and a clapper snap; a 0.5 s reset between takes (rows 1r and 2r). The counter and dot leave when the creator turns to the viewer (row 3c).
- **Shots:** 14 rows in the table (some rows are two beats of one generated clip, split where the caption changes). Hard cuts only.
- **Generated clips:** 5, 5 + 4 + 5 + 6 + 7 s each, 27 s of footage in all (21.2 s used on screen).
- **Signature sound:** The clapper snap on every jump cut, with a 2-frame white flash and a short record-beep at the start of each take. No music; room tone and the creator only.

Blank: "____" is always drawn as a thick pen-blue underline (`#2B44D6`) and never filled.

## Two alternative openings for hook tests

1. **Blank first.** Open 0.0 to 1.0 on giant type only: the headline "This video is sponsored by ____" on a yellow plate over a frozen frame of the creator mid-word, one clapper snap at 0.9, then jump cut into Take 1. All spoken words stay in order. Adds 1.0 s.
2. **Counter slam.** Open on the end of Take 1: the creator frozen on "by", the caption stopped at the blank, "TAKE 1" at triple size for 0.6 s, the flash and clapper snap, then jump cut to the top of Take 1 and run on. All spoken words stay in order. Adds 0.6 s.

All captions, labels and the blank stay the same. Changes to the order of spoken words need the owner's approval.

## Risks for AI generation and how the shots avoid them

| Risk | How the design avoids it |
|---|---|
| Same person, room and clothes across 5 clips | One character reference still (hoodie colour, hair, room), used as the start image of every clip. The jump cuts, flashes and 0.5 s resets hide small drifts, and they are meant to look like raw footage. |
| A real-looking mistake | Every take is only 4 to 5 s so the face and mouth stay stable. The "mistakes" are simple actions: a freeze with eyes flicking away, a too-fast start that trips, a stop and a held beat. If a take trips for real, that is usable footage, not a failure. |
| Mouth shapes on cut-off words | Each take ends on a closed or half-open mouth. The flash and snap cover the cut, so lip-sync never has to finish the word. |
| Words and screens in the scene | No posters, writing, logos, or readable phone or tripod screen; the phone is shown from behind. The counter, red dot, captions, chips and tags are all graphics added afterwards, so the "recording" look is never generated. |
| Hands and fingers | Only a hand fixing the hair in the resets and open palms in the offer. No pointing, no counting on fingers. |
| Speech pace | 42 words, no shot faster than 2.3 words per second (table below). The resets and the freeze carry the rest of the 21.2 s. |
| Honesty | The line is never finished in any take and no name is said. "Pretend" and "AI actor" are on from the first frame, before the first spoken word. They leave only when the creator drops the act. |

## Honesty check

- No frame shows or names a sponsor, a brand, money, a result or a celebration.
- The blank is empty in every frame including the end card.
- "AI actor" is on every row with a person. "Pretend" is on every acted sponsor moment, up before the first spoken word of it.
- The end card carries "Applying doesn't promise a sponsor."
