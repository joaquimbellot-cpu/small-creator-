Vertical 9:16 for YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels. Style: see `style.md`. All words on screen are graphics added in the edit; none is generated.

## Pitch and promise

- **Pitch:** A cook does a full, sincere sponsor read for a plain white box with nothing on it, turns it over looking for a name, and finds none.
- **Promise:** If your channel has 1,000 to 50,000 subscribers, you can apply for a sponsor, free.
- **Voice:** On-camera speech (cook, lip-synced or dubbed); no narrator.
- **Total length:** 27.2 s (24.2 s of story plus a 3.0 s end card).
- **Shots:** 9 rows in the table (some rows are two beats of one generated clip, split where the caption changes). Hard cuts only.
- **Generated clips:** 4, 8 + 5 + 7 + 8 s each, 28 s of footage in all (24.2 s used on screen).
- **Signature sound:** The hollow knock of the empty box: a dull, light cardboard-like thump on every tap, plus one small pop on "Something missing?". No music; kitchen room tone only.

Blank: "____" is always drawn as a thick pen-blue underline (`#2B44D6`) and never filled.

## Two alternative openings for hook tests

1. **Search first.** Open 0.0 to 2.0 on the close-up of the cook turning the box over and finding nothing, with the stamp and both labels up and the knock sound. Then hard cut to the start of the read (rows 1 to 2b) and run on. All words stay in order; the look-up beat is used twice.
2. **Trophy hold.** Open on the cook holding the box out at arm length like a trophy, beaming, with the stamp and a single hollow knock; the thanks line starts at 1.0 instead of 0.6. All words and their order stay.

All captions, labels and the blank stay the same. Changes to the order of spoken words need the owner's approval.

## Risks for AI generation and how the shots avoid them

| Risk | How the design avoids it |
|---|---|
| The blank box | AI likes to print logos and words on packaging. Prompt: matte white box, no print, no seams, no text, no logo. Reject any take with a mark. Any text on the box would break the whole joke. |
| Holding and turning the box | Medium shot with hands below the chest and a plain box that stays rigid. Row 3 is its own 5 s clip; if the turn warps the box, cut on the look-up and show only the face and shoulders. |
| "Comes in three colours" | No colours are shown and no fingers count to three. The line is deliberately nonsense said to a white box. |
| Kitchen physics | Counter holds a bowl and a cutting board only. No pouring, no boiling, no knife work, no liquid. |
| Same person and kitchen | One reference still; the box is the one prop that must stay identical, so it appears in a locked reference still too. |
| Honesty | The stamp and "Pretend" are on screen from 0.0, 0.6 s before the thanks line. The praise is aimed at a blank box, never at the offer. No brand, no name. |

## Honesty check

- No frame shows or names a sponsor, a brand, money, a result or a celebration.
- The blank is empty in every frame including the end card.
- "AI actor" is on every row with a person. "Pretend" is on every acted sponsor moment, up before the first spoken word of it.
- The end card carries "Applying doesn't promise a sponsor."
