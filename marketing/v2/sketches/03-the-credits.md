# 3. The Credits (score 40)

Vertical 9:16 for YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels. Style: see `style.md`. All words on screen are graphics added in the edit; none is generated.

## Pitch and promise

- **Pitch:** The end credits of a one-person channel: "Filmed by: you. Edited by: you. Sponsored by: ____".
- **Promise:** If your channel has 1,000 to 50,000 subscribers, you can apply for a sponsor, free.
- **Voice:** Deadpan off-screen narrator (AI voice). The creator does not speak, so no lip-sync is needed.
- **Total length:** 24.2 s (21.2 s of story plus a 3.0 s end card).
- **Shots:** 9 rows in the table (some rows are two beats of one generated clip, split where the caption changes). Hard cuts only.
- **Generated clips:** 4, 4 + 4 + 8 + 12 s each, 28 s of footage in all (21.2 s used on screen).
- **Signature sound:** The credits tune stopping. A bouncy, original, ukulele-and-clap credits jingle (no licensed music) starts at 0.0 and stops dead at 3.9 as the blank appears. Typewriter ticks on each credit line. One second of near silence follows.

Blank: "____" is always drawn as a thick pen-blue underline (`#2B44D6`) and never filled.

## What a muted viewer reads in the first 3 seconds

The headline "Sponsor offer. Free to apply." (large, from 0.0), tag "AI actor", credit lines "Filmed by: you." (0.2) and "Edited by: you." (1.7), and "Sponsored by: ____" (3.5, blank blinking). A muted viewer reads the offer, that it is free, and the whole joke from type alone. No "Pretend" tag is used because nobody acts a sponsor moment here; the creator only looks at a blank line.

## Two alternative openings for hook tests

1. **Type only.** Open 0.0 to 2.0 on the three credit lines typing on a plain yellow field, no picture, the last line "Sponsored by: ____" blank and blinking, tune stops dead at 1.8. Then hard cut to the creator grinning and run the ad from row 1. All words stay.
2. **Blank close-up.** Open on the creator looking up at the empty third line (row 3 shot), the tune already stopped and the caption "One line's missing." up. Then rewind to "Filmed by: you." Tests an opening on the problem. Needs approval of the spoken order if "One line's missing." is spoken first.

All captions, labels and the blank stay the same. Changes to the order of spoken words need the owner's approval.

## Risks for AI generation and how the shots avoid them

| Risk | How the design avoids it |
|---|---|
| Screens | The laptop is shown from the back only, plain lid, no logo; the camera on the tripod is in side view. No monitor, no phone screen. If the lid gets a logo, swap it for headphones and a plain notebook. |
| Words in the scene | All credits are graphics. No scrolling credits are filmed. They pop in place so the type never travels through the bottom 20%. |
| Three different jobs, one person | Rows 1 to 3 are three short clips from one reference still. Keep wardrobe and desk identical. |
| Mug | The mug sits on the desk and is never lifted or poured, because liquid physics fail in AI video. |
| Voice | A narrator removes lip-sync risk. The narrator reads exactly the script words and never speaks a brand or a name. |
| Honesty | Row 3 to the end card: the third credit line is blank in every frame, and the blink is a cursor, never a fill. |

## Honesty check

- No frame shows or names a sponsor, a brand, money, a result or a celebration.
- The blank is empty in every frame including the end card.
- "AI actor" is on every row with a person. "Pretend" is on every acted sponsor moment, up before the first spoken word of it.
- The end card carries "Applying doesn't promise a sponsor."
