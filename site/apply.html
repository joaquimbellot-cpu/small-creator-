The process

What happens next

  1. We reply

    You hear from us within 2 working days.

  2. We choose a sponsor

    A person looks at your channel and chooses a sponsor that suits it.

  3. You decide

    You can turn down any sponsor we suggest, and commission applies only once a deal is done.
