How it works

How a channel meets its sponsor

You apply, we choose a sponsor for your channel, and you decide whether to accept.

  1. You apply

    Fill out the application with a link to your channel, what it is about and your subscriber count.

    We work with channels of 1,000 to 50,000 subscribers, and a channel under 1,000 cannot join yet.

  2. We choose a sponsor

    A person looks at your channel and chooses a sponsor that suits it.

    No automatic list, no algorithm.

  3. You decide

    We tell you which sponsor we have chosen, and you say yes or no. You can turn down any sponsor we suggest.

    The sponsor approves each channel we suggest to it.

  4. The deal is done

    When you and the sponsor both say yes, the deal goes ahead.

    This is the point at which our commission applies.

After you apply

What to expect from us

  • A reply from us

    We reply within 2 working days of your application.

  • No searching on your side

    You do not look for sponsors yourself. Finding one that suits your channel is our work.

  • A way to ask questions

    Write to us by email at any point. The address is on the contact page.

Commission

What we charge, and when

The commission is taken from your fee, and only once a deal is done. With no deal, there is no commission.
