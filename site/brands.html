What we offer

Chosen by a person, approved by you

  • Channels chosen by hand

    A person looks at each channel before we suggest it. No automatic list, no algorithm.

  • Only small channels

    Every channel we work with is on YouTube and has 1,000 to 50,000 subscribers.

  • You approve each channel

    You see every channel we suggest, and none goes ahead without your approval.

Three steps

How it works for a brand

  1. You send an inquiry

    Tell us your company, what you want to promote and your budget range.

  2. We suggest channels

    We come back with small YouTube channels that suit what you want to promote.

  3. You approve

    You approve the channels you want, and a deal goes ahead once the creator says yes too.

Cost

You pay the agreed fee and nothing more

Our commission is taken from the creator’s fee, and only once a deal is done.
