Questions and answers

If your question is not answered here, write to us by email.

small.fold.sponsorship@gmail.com

Joining

Who can join Smallfold?

Creators with a YouTube channel of 1,000 to 50,000 subscribers. A channel with fewer than 1,000 subscribers cannot join yet.

How do I apply?

Fill out the application form. We ask for your name, your email, a link to your channel, what it is about and your subscriber count. A phone number is optional.

What happens after I apply?

We reply within 2 working days. A person looks at your channel and chooses a sponsor that suits it, and we suggest that sponsor to you.

Which platforms do you work with?

YouTube, for now. Other platforms will come later.

How do I contact you?

Write to us by email. The address is on the contact page and at the bottom of every page.

Sponsors and matches

Do I have to look for sponsors myself?

No. Finding the sponsor is our work. Your part is to apply, and to say yes or no to what we suggest.

How do you choose a sponsor for my channel?

By hand. A person looks at your channel and picks a sponsor that suits it. There is no automatic list and no algorithm.

Can I say no to a sponsor?

Yes. You can turn down any sponsor we suggest. The final say is yours.

Does the sponsor have a say?

Yes. A sponsor approves each channel we suggest to it. A deal goes ahead only when you and the sponsor both say yes.

I represent a brand. How do I start?

Send a short inquiry from the For brands page. Tell us what you want to promote and your budget range.

Commission

What does Smallfold cost?

Joining is free. We take a commission from your fee once a deal is done. It is 10% on a sponsorship under $500, 8% from $500 to $1,999, and 5% at $2,000 or more.

When is the commission charged?

Only once a deal is done. If there is no deal, there is no commission.

Do sponsors pay extra because of the commission?

No. The sponsor pays exactly the agreed fee and nothing extra. The commission comes out of your fee.
