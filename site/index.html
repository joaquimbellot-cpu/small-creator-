For channels with 1,000 to 50,000 subscribers.

Smallfold was started by a YouTube creator with a small channel who found that sponsors only looked at big ones. So we look at the small ones. A person looks at each channel and chooses a sponsor that suits it.

Why Smallfold

Three things we hold to

  • Matched by hand

    No automatic list, no algorithm. Every sponsor we suggest is picked by a person.

  • Only small channels

    We work only with channels that have 1,000 to 50,000 subscribers.

  • You have the final say

    You can turn down any sponsor we suggest. Nothing goes ahead without your yes.

The process

How a match is made

From your application to a deal, in four steps.

  1. You apply

    Send us a link to your channel, what it is about and your subscriber count.

  2. We choose a sponsor

    We look at your channel and choose a sponsor to suggest.

  3. You decide

    You say yes or no to the sponsor, and the sponsor says yes or no to your channel.

  4. The deal is done

    With a yes on both sides, the deal goes ahead.

Questions

Before you apply

  • Do I have to look for sponsors myself?

    No. Finding the sponsor is our work. Your part is to apply, and to say yes or no to what we suggest.

  • What if my channel has fewer than 1,000 subscribers?

    It cannot join yet. We work with channels of 1,000 to 50,000 subscribers, so apply once yours reaches 1,000.

  • Is Smallfold only for YouTube?

    For now, yes. Other platforms will come later.
