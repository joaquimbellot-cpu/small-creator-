About

We look at the small ones

Smallfold finds sponsors for small YouTube channels, with a person choosing each one by hand.

Our story

It began with a small channel

Smallfold was started by a YouTube creator with a small channel who found that sponsors only looked at big ones.

That finding is why Smallfold exists. Today we find sponsors for small YouTube channels, and that is all we do.

What we believe

Three beliefs behind every match

  • A person should choose

    A sponsor should be chosen by someone who has looked at the channel. So a person does it, with no automatic list and no algorithm.

  • Small channels deserve attention

    We work only with channels of 1,000 to 50,000 subscribers. Here a small channel is never behind a big one.

  • The decision belongs to you

    It is your channel. You can turn down any sponsor we suggest, and nothing goes ahead without your yes.

Platforms

YouTube now, other platforms later

Today we work with YouTube channels only. Other platforms will come later.
