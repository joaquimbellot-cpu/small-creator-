Privacy policy

This page explains which details we collect through this website, what we use them for and what you can ask of us.

Updated 20 November 2026

Who we are

This website is run by Smallfold, which finds sponsors for small YouTube channels. Smallfold is not a registered business yet. Its legal name is (legal name to be added) and it is based in (country to be added). In this policy, “we” means Smallfold.

What we collect

If you apply as a creator, the application form asks for your name, your email address, a link to your YouTube channel, what the channel is about and its subscriber count. A phone number is optional.

If you send a brand inquiry, the form asks for your company, what you want to promote, a budget range, your name and your email address. A phone number is optional.

If you write to us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to tell us.

How we use your details

We use the details in an application to look at your channel, to reply to you and to find a sponsor that suits your channel.

We use the details in a brand inquiry to reply to you and to suggest channels that suit what you want to promote.

Services that handle your details

Each form is delivered to our email inbox through a form-handling service (name of the form service to be added). Your details pass through that service on the way to us. Our inbox is a Gmail account, provided by Google.

This site loads its fonts from Google Fonts. To fetch them, your browser contacts Google’s servers, and Google receives your IP address as part of that request.

Cookies

This site sets no advertising cookies and no analytics cookies.

The opening animation on the home page plays once per visit. To do that, the site keeps one small note in your browser’s session storage, which holds no personal details and is removed when you close the browser tab.
