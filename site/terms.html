Terms of service

These terms describe what Smallfold does, who can apply, what it costs and what each side decides.

Updated 20 November 2026

Who we are

Smallfold finds sponsors for small YouTube channels. Smallfold is not a registered business yet. Its legal name is (legal name to be added) and it is based in (country to be added).

In these terms, “we” means Smallfold. A “creator” is a person who applies with a YouTube channel. A “sponsor” is a brand that wants to sponsor a channel.

What we do

A person looks at each channel and chooses a sponsor that suits it. We suggest the sponsor to the creator and the channel to the sponsor. For now we work with YouTube channels only.

We do not promise to find a sponsor for every channel, or a channel for every sponsor.

Who can apply

A creator can apply with a YouTube channel that has 1,000 to 50,000 subscribers. A channel with fewer than 1,000 subscribers cannot join yet.

Applying is free, and joining is free. We reply to an application within 2 working days.

The details in an application or a brand inquiry must be true, including the subscriber count.

Who decides

A creator can turn down any sponsor we suggest. A sponsor approves each channel we suggest to it. A deal goes ahead only when the creator and the sponsor both agree.

How each deal is agreed and put in writing is (deal agreement to be added).

Commission

We charge a commission only once a deal is done. The commission is taken from the creator’s fee. If there is no deal, there is no commission.

The rate depends on the size of the sponsorship. It is 10% under $500, 8% from $500 to $1,999, and 5% at $2,000 or more.

The sponsor pays exactly the agreed fee and nothing extra.
