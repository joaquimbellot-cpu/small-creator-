Every component of the site: live on each ground, with the markup to copy and one line on when to use it.

  • Copy the markup as it is. A component is never restyled in a page; if one does not fit, tell the Design System owner.

  • Each ground sets six variables: --bg, --fg, --fg-muted, --rule, --surface, --accent-fg. Styles for a page section use these six, never a colour token directly, and they work on every ground.
  • .panel is always on ink and .band is always on a photo; they need no ground class of their own.

Colour tokens

For reference. Pages use the six ground variables above; only tokens.css names these.

  • --cream #F1EFEB. The page ground.
  • --paper #FAF8F4. Raised surfaces on cream.
  • --cream-deep #E5E1D8. A deeper cream. No component reads it.
  • --ink #0E0E0E. Text on cream. The dark ground.
  • --ink-soft #1A1A19. Raised surfaces on ink.
  • --muted #5C5A55. Secondary text on cream, 6.0 to 1.
  • --muted-on-ink #A9A59C. Secondary text on ink, 7.9 to 1.
  • --faint #B9B5AC. Decoration only. Never text to read.
  • --line 14% ink. Hairlines on cream.
  • --line-on-ink 18% cream. Hairlines on ink.
  • --accent #2B44D6. Pen blue on cream, 6.3 to 1.
  • --accent-on-ink #A8B4FF. Pen blue on ink, 9.8 to 1.
  • --veil 64% ink. Laid over a photo that carries text.

02

Layout

Two widths and one rhythm. Every section of a page is built from these four classes.

.section .section--tight .wrap .wrap--narrow

.section gives a block of the page its space above and below. .wrap centres the content at 1320px with the gutter; .wrap--narrow is the 720px text column.

.wrap: 1320px, plus the gutter at both sides

.wrap--narrow: 720px

<section class="section">
  <div class="wrap">
    ...
  </div>
</section>

<!-- Less space above and below, and the narrow text column -->
<section class="section section--tight">
  <div class="wrap wrap--narrow">
    ...
  </div>
</section>
  • Sections are 112px apart on a wide screen and 72px on a phone (--section). .section--tight uses 72px and 48px.
  • Two cream sections in a row share one gap: the second one loses its top padding by itself. A section on ink or on a photo keeps both.
  • Inside a section, set distances with the spacing tokens --s-1 to --s-10 (4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 72, 112, 160px).
  • The phone layout starts at 860px and below. Corners: --r-sm 4px, --r 8px, --r-lg 14px.

Accessibility

Two classes and one rule that every page relies on.

.sr-only .skip-link and the focus ring

.sr-only keeps words in the page for screen readers without showing them. .skip-link is the first link of every page; press Tab at the top of this page to see it.

<!-- Already in the layout of every page -->
<a class="skip-link" href="#main">{{site.labels.skip}}</a>

<!-- A name for something that shows no words -->
<span class="sr-only">{{site.labels.close}}</span>
  • Whatever has the keyboard shows a 2px ring in the pen blue, 3px away from it. It is built in for links, buttons, fields, choice tiles and questions: never remove an outline.
  • On a photo the gap inside the ring is filled with ink, so the ring shows on any picture.
  • Everything that can be tapped is at least 44px tall.
  • Hover effects apply only where a pointer can hover; pressed states work on touch screens too.
  • With reduced motion, every transition and animation is cut to nothing.
