How it works

How we find your sponsor

  1. Tell us about your channel

    Join as a creator and send us the link to your YouTube channel.

  2. We look for a sponsor

    We look at your videos and your audience, and find a sponsor that suits them.

  3. You say yes or no

    We tell you who the sponsor is, what they want in the video and what they pay. You decide.

  4. Publish and get paid

    You publish the video, and the sponsor pays your fee through Smallfold.

Questions

Before you join

Do I look for the sponsor myself?

No. You tell us about your channel and we look for a sponsor that suits it. You do not apply to a list of offers.

What does Smallfold cost?

Nothing for creators. Smallfold takes a 10% commission on each deal, and the sponsor pays it. You keep the whole fee. No deal, no commission.

Is my channel big enough?

Yes, if it has between 1,000 and 50,000 subscribers. A channel with fewer than 1,000 cannot join yet: come back when you reach 1,000.

Can I say no to a sponsor?

Yes. We suggest a sponsor and you decide. Nothing is agreed without you.

How do I get paid?

Through Smallfold. The sponsor pays your fee there once your video is published.
